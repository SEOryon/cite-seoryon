HOW THIS IS SCORED

A heuristic, shown in the open.

This is not a guarantee of citation — no tool can promise that. It's a transparent estimate based on the structural content factors that research ties to being cited by AI engines. Studies of generative-engine and answer-engine optimization find that a handful of these factors explain most of the difference between content that gets quoted and content that gets skipped. We score each one and weight them into a single 0–100 grade.

Answer-first

AI engines lift the answer from near the top. We check whether your content leads with a direct answer instead of a long wind-up — and, if you gave a target question, whether it's answered early.

Q&A / FAQ structure

Question-shaped headings with self-contained answers are cited far more often. We detect question headings and whether each is followed by a clean, standalone answer.

Extractable passages & data

Quotable, self-contained sentences and concrete numbers and stats give an engine something to cite. Vague, context-dependent prose gives it nothing to lift.

Scannability & clarity

Clear headings, lists and short declarative sentences help. Readability is scored with a standard formula (English-tuned; for DE/FR we fall back to a language-agnostic length measure and say so).

The weights are documented in the tool's source and shown per factor in your result. Factor selection follows the public findings behind generative-engine optimization (GEO) and answer-engine optimization (AEO) — the same rules used by open-source auditors like Auriti-Labs/geo-optimizer and Canonry/aeo-audit. We reimplemented the rules in our own code; we round every score so it never fakes precision.

Questions, answered

Is the Citability Score a guarantee my content will be cited?

No — and any tool that claims to guarantee AI citation is bluffing. The score is a heuristic estimate based on the structural factors research ties to being cited (answer-first phrasing, Q&A structure, extractable passages, data, scannability, clarity). It tells you how well your writing is shaped to be quoted; whether an engine actually quotes it also depends on your topic, authority, and competition.

How is this different from score.seoryon.com?

They're companions. score.seoryon.com checks a live URL's technical AI-search health — crawlability, meta tags, schema, on-page signals. This tool grades the writing itself — is the prose structured and phrased to be quoted. One is the page layer, one is the content layer. Use both.

What exactly does it check?

Seven content factors: whether you answer up front, Q&A / FAQ structure, whether passages are self-contained and extractable, statistical / data density, heading and list scannability, readability, and the presence of short direct-answer sentences. Each gets a pass / warn / fail with a one-line reason, and every failing factor becomes a fix in your list.

Does it send my content anywhere?

No. There is no server, no API, no upload. The scoring runs as plain JavaScript in your browser, and your text never leaves the page. The only thing stored is your language choice, kept locally in your browser.

Does it work for German and French content?

Yes. The structural checks (answer-first, question headings, self-contained passages, data density, scannability) are language-agnostic. Readability uses an English-tuned formula for English and a language-agnostic length measure for German and French — we flag which one was used in your result so nothing is hidden.

Why does the same text always get the same score?

Because the scoring is deterministic rules, not an AI model. The same content always produces the same grade and the same fix-list — so you can edit, re-paste, and watch the score move as you improve it.
